Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure? ➕

Yes, we use AES-256 encryption, the same standard used by banks and government agencies. We operate on a zero-knowledge architecture, meaning even we cannot access your encrypted files.

How does it work with Time Machine? ➕

MacCloud Pro integrates seamlessly with Time Machine. You can back up directly to your cloud storage or use it as an additional backup destination alongside local drives.

Can I access files from iPhone or iPad? ➕

Absolutely. We have native iOS and iPadOS apps that sync perfectly with your MacBook. Files are available across all your Apple devices.

What happens after the free trial? ➕

After 14 days, you can choose any paid plan. If you don't subscribe, your account will be downgraded to a free plan with 5GB storage and basic features.

Is there a file size limit? ➕

No file size limits on any plan. Upload and sync files of any size, from small documents to large video projects.
